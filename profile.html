人生の大きな転換期。
都立に落ちて「高い志を持つ優秀な生徒」が多く、彼らに強く感化された。友人との小テスト勝負に負けた悔しさをバネに闘争心に火がつき、高校の最終評定を3.9から4.1へ（＋0.2）上昇させ、一つ上のクラスへのステップアップを見事に果たしました。
A major turning point in my life.
After failing to enter a public high school, I was deeply inspired by many "excellent students with high aspirations" here. The frustration of losing a quiz competition to a friend ignited my competitive spirit. I successfully raised my final GPA from 3.9 to 4.1 (+0.2), brilliantly achieving a step up to a higher-level class.
